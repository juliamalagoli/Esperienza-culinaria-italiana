Proposals

The perfect setting to propose.

An intimate, candlelit Italian dinner at home — private, unhurried, and quietly perfect for the biggest question of all.

When the moment matters this much, privacy is everything. Rather than a crowded restaurant, I create an intimate Italian dinner in your own home — candlelight, a beautiful table, and a menu chosen to make the evening feel like yours alone.

I cook and serve discreetly in the background, so the two of you are never interrupted. Tell me your plan and I'll help time the evening around your moment — and celebrate with you after.

Every detail, from the aperitivo to the dessert, is designed so all you have to think about is the question.
