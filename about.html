About

The chef at your table.

An Italian cook, a London kitchen for the night, and a love of doing things the traditional way.

IAYTPortrait of the chef
Piacere — nice to meet you

Bringing the heart of Italy to your table.

My name is Julia and I am an Italian chef based in London.

I was born and raised in Modena, a city celebrated around the world for its extraordinary culinary heritage. Growing up in Emilia-Romagna, I learned that food is much more than something we eat — it's a way of bringing people together, celebrating life's moments, and creating lasting memories.

Everything I know about traditional Italian cooking began in my grandmothers' kitchens.

Some of my happiest childhood memories are of standing beside them, learning how to roll fresh pasta by hand, tasting slow-cooked ragù, and discovering that the most meaningful recipes are passed down through generations, not written in books.

Those moments shaped the way I cook today. Every dish I prepare carries the values they taught me: simplicity, patience, respect for seasonal ingredients, and above all, cooking with love.

Today, I bring those traditions into homes across London through intimate private dining experiences. Rather than simply preparing a meal, I create an evening where guests can slow down, gather around the table, and experience the warmth of genuine Italian hospitality.

From a welcome aperitivo and handmade dishes to carefully chosen seasonal ingredients, every detail is designed to make you feel as though you've been invited into an Italian home.

Whether you're celebrating a special occasion or simply want to share an unforgettable evening with family and friends, my goal is always the same.

What I believe

A few things I won't compromise on.

Fresh, always

I shop the day of your dinner and cook everything in your kitchen. No pre-made trays, no shortcuts — just ingredients at their best.

Traditional at heart

Hand-made pasta, slow ragù, a tiramisù built the proper way. Recipes with roots, cooked with respect for how they should taste.

Warmth over formality

An Italian table is relaxed and generous. I want your guests to feel looked after, never on show.
