I'm [your name], an Italian chef living and working in London. I grew up in [your region / town in Italy], where the day was shaped around the table and the best recipes were never written down — they were taught by [who taught you: nonna, mother, family, a kitchen you trained in].

When I moved to London [in year / how you came here], I noticed how many people love Italian food but rarely get to experience it the way it's meant to be: unhurried, generous, made from scratch and eaten among friends. So I started cooking it for them — in their own homes.

Italy at Your Table is that idea, made simple. I bring the market, the kitchen skills and the hospitality; you bring the people you want to spend the evening with.

What I believe

A few things I won't compromise on.

Fresh, always

I shop the day of your dinner and cook everything in your kitchen. No pre-made trays, no shortcuts — just ingredients at their best.

Traditional at heart

Hand-made pasta, slow ragù, a tiramisù built the proper way. Recipes with roots, cooked with respect for how they should taste.

Warmth over formality

An Italian table is relaxed and generous. I want your guests to feel looked after, never on show.
