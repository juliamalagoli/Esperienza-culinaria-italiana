Birthdays

A birthday dinner they'll remember.

Mark the occasion with a private Italian chef at home — a menu built around the guest of honour, and not a single dish to wash.

Birthdays are for the people around the table, not for booking restaurants and rushing home. I bring the celebration to you: a welcome aperitivo, a menu shaped around the birthday guest, and a proper Italian dolce to finish — candles optional but encouraged.

Whether it's an intimate dinner or a table of friends and family, everything is cooked fresh in your kitchen and served course by course while you enjoy the party.

Ask me about a celebration dessert and birthday candles when we plan — a lovely way to mark the moment.
