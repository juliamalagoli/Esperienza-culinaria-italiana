The Experience

Everything handled. You just host.

From the first message to the last coffee, here's how an evening with Italy at Your Table unfolds.

We plan it together

You tell me the occasion, the date, how many guests and any dietary needs. We choose a menu — one of my sample menus, or something shaped around you.

I shop fresh

On the day, I hand-pick the ingredients: the ripe tomatoes, the right cut, the good olive oil. Freshness is the whole point.

I come to you

I arrive at your home, set up in your kitchen and get to work — so the aromas of an Italian dinner fill your home, not a restaurant's.

Aperitivo & antipasti

Your guests are welcomed with an Italian aperitivo and warm bites while I cook. The evening starts relaxed, with a glass in hand.

Dinner, served course by course

I plate and serve each course to the table, at your pace. You stay in your seat and enjoy the company.

A spotless kitchen

When dinner's done, I clean everything down and leave your kitchen exactly as I found it. The night is yours to linger in.

Before dinner

The welcome aperitivo.

The Italian way to begin: a drink, something to nibble, and no rush. While I finish in the kitchen, your guests settle in with a proper aperitivo.

To drink

Aperol Spritz · Pimm's Cup · Prosecco · Italian wine · an alcohol-free Italian spritz.

To nibble

Homemade focaccia with cherry tomato · marinated olives · and a mini starter matched to your menu — a courgette omelette, a ham and cheese omelette, or prawns in Marie Rose sauce.
