Anniversaries

An anniversary dinner, just the two of you.

No booking a table, no rushing home — an intimate Italian dinner cooked in your own kitchen and served just for you.

Some evenings deserve more than a restaurant. For your anniversary, I bring an authentic Italian dinner into your home — candlelight, an aperitivo in hand, and course after course cooked fresh while you stay exactly where you want to be: together.

You choose the menu (or leave it to me), and I take care of everything from the shopping to the washing-up. It's the romance of a fine-dining evening, in the comfort and privacy of home.

Tell me your date and I'll help shape a menu worth remembering — perhaps the burrata to start, hand-made pasta, and a tiramisù to finish.

How it works

Your evening, handled from start to finish.

We plan it together

A relaxed chat on WhatsApp: your date, your guests, the menu and any dietary needs.

I shop & cook fresh

I hand-pick the ingredients on the day and cook everything in your kitchen — nothing pre-made.

Aperitivo & dinner served

Guests are welcomed with an Italian aperitivo, then I serve dinner course by course, at your pace.

A spotless kitchen

When the evening winds down, I clean everything and leave — only the memory stays.
