Not catering. Not meal prep.

A dinner that feels like Italy — without leaving home.

I'm an Italian chef living in London, and I cook the food I grew up with: honest, seasonal, made by hand. This isn't a drop-off service or a stack of trays. It's an evening — one where I arrive at your home, cook everything fresh in your kitchen, and serve you and your guests course by course.

When I leave, the only thing left behind is the memory of a good Italian dinner. The kitchen is spotless.

My story
How the evening unfolds

From aperitivo to the last spoonful.

Every detail is handled, so your only job is to enjoy the company at your table.

The welcome aperitivo

Guests arrive to an Italian-style aperitivo — a spritz or a glass of prosecco, focaccia still warm, marinated olives and little bites — while I finish in the kitchen.

Dinner, served

A menu chosen with you and cooked fresh: antipasti, hand-made pasta or a seasonal main, and a proper Italian dolce to close. Served to the table, at your pace.

A clean kitchen

I shop, I cook, I serve — and before I leave, I clean everything down. You wake up to a tidy home and a very good memory.
