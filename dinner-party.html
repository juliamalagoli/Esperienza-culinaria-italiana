Dinner parties

Host the dinner party without the work.

Gather your friends around your table and stay a guest at your own party — I handle the shopping, cooking, serving and clean-up.

The best dinner parties are the ones where the host actually enjoys them. I bring an authentic Italian evening to your home so you can pour the wine and sit down with your guests, not disappear into the kitchen.

Guests arrive to an Italian aperitivo, then I serve a hand-made menu course by course — vegetarian, meat or fish, or a mix. When the night winds down, I clean everything and slip away.

Perfect for friends getting together, family gatherings and small celebrations across central and north London.
